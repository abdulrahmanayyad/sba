Critical notes:
- Jordan is both an import market and a major apparel/garment manufacturing exporter.
- HS61/HS62 imports and Egypt bilateral values are from UN Comtrade series as presented by Trading Economics.
- Product-level HS6114 CEPII-BACI value ($10.1m Egypt) conflicts with the chapter-level UN Comtrade series ($30.32m Other knitted garments). The report flags this and does not reconcile without a unified extraction.
- Jordan's 2025 sector exports (JOD 1.774bn) cover leather and garments sector, not only HS61/62.
- Buyer entities are prospects, not confirmed customers.
- Jordan's standard sales tax is 16%; exact customs duties vary by HS line and origin.
- US-Jordan FTA/QIZ origin rules are separate from GAFTA/Agadir and must not be confused.
