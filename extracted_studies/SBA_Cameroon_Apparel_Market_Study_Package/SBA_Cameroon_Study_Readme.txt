IMPORTANT NOTES
- ECCAS CET entered into force in Cameroon on 1 January 2026. Verify exact tariff line
  in the current official schedule; do not rely solely on legacy CEMAC rates.
- WITS supplier-to-Cameroon data are partner export figures, not necessarily the
  complete Cameroon-reported import value.
- The USD59.8m women's clothing figure is CEPII-BACI HS2017 and must not be merged
  with WITS HS H0 figures without harmonization.
- Cameroon Customs prior formalities indicate DI at FOB XAF1m+ and RVC at XAF2m+.
- PECAE/ANOR conformity scope and method must be confirmed for each exact garment.
- Buyer leads from Volza are paid-data leads and require company/KYC validation.
- This is commercial research, not legal/customs advice.

Full source URLs are in the report and workbook.
