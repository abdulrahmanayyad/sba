SAMI BIN AYYAD GROUP – POLAND APPAREL MARKET STUDY PACKAGE
Contents:
1) SBA_Poland_Apparel_Market_Study_AR.docx – Arabic market study.
2) SBA_Poland_Apparel_Market_Data.xlsx – market indicators, sector opportunities, used clothing HS630900, prospects, compliance checklist and sources.

Data basis:
- New apparel market indicators: 2024, CBI analysis of Eurostat.
- Used clothing: HS630900, 2024, WITS/UN Comtrade.
- Outerwear and workwear figures are separate product-market indicators and are not added to total apparel imports.
- Prospects are potential outreach targets, not confirmed buyers or active purchase leads.

Important:
- Do not substitute HS392620 or HS401590 for textile apparel HS61/HS62.
- Confirm exact CN/TARIC classification, duty and preferential origin rules for each SKU.
- Used clothing HS630900 is not included in new clothing market size.
