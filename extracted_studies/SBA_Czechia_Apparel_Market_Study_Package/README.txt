SAMI BIN AYYAD GROUP – CZECHIA APPAREL MARKET STUDY PACKAGE

Contents:
1) SBA_Czechia_Apparel_Market_Study_AR.docx – Arabic market study.
2) SBA_Czechia_Apparel_Market_Data.xlsx – market indicators, HS6 product imports, sector opportunities, used clothing HS630900, prospects, compliance checklist and sources.

Data basis:
- Eurostat SITC 84 imports: 2024; broader classification, not identical to HS61+HS62.
- CBI intra-EU exports and growth: 2024.
- Outerwear and workwear: CBI/Eurostat latest available figures.
- HS610910, HS620342 and HS630900: WITS/UN Comtrade, 2024.
- Prospects are potential outreach targets, not confirmed buyers.

Important:
- Egypt has recorded exports in HS610910 and HS620342; these are specific tariff lines, not total apparel exports.
- Do not use HS392620 or HS401590 as substitutes for textile apparel HS61/HS62.
- Used clothing HS630900 is separate from new apparel.
- Confirm exact CN/TARIC, preferential origin, labelling, REACH, GPSR, packaging and EPR obligations for each SKU.
