SAMI BIN AYYAD GROUP – GREECE APPAREL MARKET STUDY PACKAGE

Contents:
1) SBA_Greece_Apparel_Market_Study_AR.docx – Arabic market study.
2) SBA_Greece_Apparel_Market_Data.xlsx – market indicators, HS6 product imports, workwear/uniform channels, used clothing HS630900, prospects, compliance checklist and sources.

Data basis:
- Eurostat SITC 84 imports: 2024; broader classification, not identical to HS61+HS62.
- HS610910, HS620342, HS611020 and HS630900: WITS/UN Comtrade, 2024.
- Prospects are potential outreach targets, not confirmed buyers.

Important:
- Egypt has recorded imports into Greece in HS610910 and HS620342; these are specific tariff lines, not total apparel exports.
- Workwear/uniform channel opportunities are identified by company profiles; no unverified market total is stated.
- Used clothing HS630900 is separate from new apparel.
- Confirm CN/TARIC, origin, Greek labelling, REACH, GPSR, VAT, packaging EPR and textile EPR for each SKU.
