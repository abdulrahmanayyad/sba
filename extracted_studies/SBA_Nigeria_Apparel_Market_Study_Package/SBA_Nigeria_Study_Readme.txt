IMPORTANT LEGAL / DATA NOTES
- Trade values are selected HS6 lines reported for 2024, not the full Nigerian apparel market.
- HS610910 cotton T-shirt imports: USD 1.692m; HS610990 other-fibre T-shirts: USD 802.47k;
  HS621010 nonwoven garments: USD 1.764m. Do not aggregate these as total apparel imports.
- Egypt's recorded values in HS610990 and HS621010 are product-line-specific.
- Used clothing HS630900 is prohibited effective 1 May 2026 according to the WTO notification.
- A historical Nigeria Trade Hub prohibition list refers to a wider range of made-up garments.
  A revised 2026 fiscal policy and prohibition list was announced. Therefore, obtain written
  confirmation from Nigeria Customs / importer customs broker for every exact HS8/HS10 code
  before shipment. This report does not declare all new garments prohibited or automatically allowed.
- ECOWAS CET bands are 0/5/10/20/35%; exact rates and IAT must be checked in the 2026 tariff.
- SONCAP applies only to regulated products; verify scope for each exact product.
- Target accounts and market clusters are prospects/channels, not confirmed importers.
- This is commercial research, not legal or customs advice.

Full sources are listed in the Word report and Excel Source Register.
