SAMI BIN AYYAD GROUP – AUSTRIA APPAREL MARKET STUDY PACKAGE

Contents:
1) SBA_Austria_Apparel_Market_Study_AR.docx – Arabic market study.
2) SBA_Austria_Apparel_Market_Data.xlsx – market indicators, HS6 product imports, outerwear/workwear, used clothing HS630900, prospects, compliance checklist and sources.

Data basis:
- CBI/Eurostat apparel market indicators: 2024.
- Eurostat SITC 84 imports: 2024; broader classification, not identical to HS61+HS62.
- HS620342, HS610910 and HS630900: WITS/UN Comtrade, 2024.
- Industry turnover/employment/export quota: Advantage Austria / Austrian industry association, 2025.
- Prospects are potential outreach targets, not confirmed buyers.

Important:
- Developing-country share of Austria's apparel imports is relatively low; assess entry selectively.
- Workwear market value not stated where a verified comparable figure was unavailable.
- Used clothing HS630900 is separate from new apparel.
- Confirm exact CN/TARIC, preferential origin, German labelling, REACH, GPSR, VAT, packaging EPR and textile EPR obligations for each SKU.
