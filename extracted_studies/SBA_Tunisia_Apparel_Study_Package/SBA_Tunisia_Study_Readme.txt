Important:
- Tunisia imports HS61 USD124.66m and HS62 USD246.78m in 2024.
- Imports from Egypt HS61 USD369.01k and HS62 USD1.62m; combined Egypt share about 0.54%.
- Tunisia is a major garment exporter: INS 2025 apparel industry exports TND5.1888bn; hosiery TND2.1077bn; woven fabrics TND1.4276bn.
- APII counts 1,275 textile/apparel enterprises with 10+ workers, 1,032 fully exporting, 143,617 jobs.
- Egypt-Tunisia bilateral FTA provides preferences subject to origin, direct transport and negative lists. Do not assume 0% without HS-specific confirmation.
- Trade.gov notes some apparel tariffs increased to 50%; official Tunisian Tarif Web 2026 must be checked for every HS8/11. VAT 19%, customs formality fee 3% of duties.
- Some HS lines are classified non-priority and may require importer self-funding under BCT 2026-4; check exact HS line.
- Tunisia is primarily an industrial partner/competitor, not a high-priority mass consumer destination.
