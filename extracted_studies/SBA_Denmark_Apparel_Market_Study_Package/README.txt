SAMI BIN AYYAD GROUP – DENMARK APPAREL MARKET STUDY PACKAGE

Contents:
1) SBA_Denmark_Apparel_Market_Study_AR.docx – Arabic market study.
2) SBA_Denmark_Apparel_Market_Data.xlsx – market indicators, supplier trends, HS6 product imports, workwear, used clothing HS630900, prospects, compliance checklist and sources.

Data scope:
- CBI/Eurostat sector apparel market figure: 2023.
- Eurostat SITC 84 imports: 2024; broader/different classification and not directly comparable to HS61+HS62.
- HS6 product examples: 2024 WITS/UN Comtrade.
- Used clothing: HS630900, 2024.
- Prospects are potential targets only, not confirmed buyers.

Important:
- Never treat SITC 84 as identical to HS61+HS62.
- HS6 examples are specific tariff lines, not total market size.
- Verify CN/TARIC, preferential origin, labelling, REACH, GPSR, packaging EPR and textile EPR status for each product and transaction.
