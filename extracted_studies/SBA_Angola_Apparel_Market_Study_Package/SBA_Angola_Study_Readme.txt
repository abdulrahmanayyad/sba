IMPORTANT NOTES
- HS6 values are individual product lines, not total HS61+HS62 apparel imports.
- Egypt recorded USD780 in Angola's 2024 HS610910 imports.
- Used clothing HS630900 imports were USD34.118m in 2024 and are separate from new apparel.
- General import duty references range 2-50%; use the enacted 2026 tariff schedule for exact HS8/10.
- VAT standard rate is 14%.
- Confirm REI, PICE, Documento Único, ADV, CNCA and any product-specific import licence before shipment.
- Pre-shipment inspection is not generally mandatory under Decree 63/13, but verify exceptions and buyer/LC requirements.
- Target brands are partnership prospects, not confirmed importers or buyers.
- This is commercial research, not legal/customs advice.

Full source URLs and notes are included in the Word report and Excel Source Register.
