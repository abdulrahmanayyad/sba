RESEARCH NOTES
- South Africa's CTFL import figure of approximately USD 4bn is a broad sector estimate
  (clothing, textiles and footwear), not an apparel-only HS61+HS62 figure.
- HS6 data in the workbook are selected product lines with source-linked values.
  They must not be summed and presented as the entire apparel import market.
- Egypt-to-South Africa 2025 HS61 and HS62 values are reported via UN Comtrade on
  secondary data pages; verify reporter/partner and HS definitions before using for
  market-share or investment claims.
- The 2024 HS611490 Egypt share is calculated only within that single HS6 line.
- South African apparel tariffs are highly HS-specific. Certain lines can reach 45%;
  other tariff lines differ. Verify the current SARS schedule for the exact product.
- AfCFTA preference is not assumed for Egyptian garments; confirm product-specific
  rules of origin and current implementation status in writing.
- Target buyers are prospects, not confirmed current importers or buyers.
- Supplier onboarding and audit requirements are buyer-specific and can change.
