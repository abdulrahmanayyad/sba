Critical data notes
- Algeria apparel imports of USD 822m in 2024 are sourced from a specialist Africa apparel trade report. They are not retail sales.
- Qoovee's USD 1.7bn Apparel & Textile category is much broader than HS61+62 and includes textiles, fabrics and other chapters. Do not equate it with ready-made apparel imports.
- Egypt-reported exports to Algeria in 2025: HS61 USD 2.28m and HS62 USD 2.69m. These are not Algeria-reported imports and do not establish Egypt's market share.
- Detailed Algeria HS61+62 importer-reporter data by supplier, HS6/HS8 duties, and garment-specific landed costs remain to be verified.
- Bank domiciliation before shipment is a critical gate. Instruction 05-2026 adds financial standing requirements for importers of goods intended for resale as-is.
- Buyer accounts are prospects only. Validate CNRC registration, current activity, bank, financial standing, buying authority and recent imports.
- VAT standard rate is 19%; reduced rate 9% applies only to specified categories. Confirm product-specific treatment.
- The $6/pc scenario input is a placeholder only, not a market price or forecast.
