SAMI BIN AYYAD GROUP – PORTUGAL APPAREL MARKET STUDY PACKAGE

Contents:
1) SBA_Portugal_Apparel_Market_Study_AR.docx – Arabic market study.
2) SBA_Portugal_Apparel_Market_Data.xlsx – market indicators, HS6 product imports, workwear, manufacturing sector, used clothing HS630900, prospects/partners, compliance checklist and sources.

Data basis:
- Eurostat SITC 84 imports: 2024; broader classification, not identical to HS61+HS62.
- HS610910, HS620342 and HS630900: WITS/UN Comtrade, 2024.
- Workwear: CBI/Eurostat 2024.
- Industry/sector figures: AICEP/Portugal Global and ATP/INE, 2024–2025.
- Prospects are potential outreach targets or industrial partners, not confirmed buyers.

Important:
- Portugal is a major apparel/textile manufacturing competitor and potential partner, not just an import market.
- Used clothing HS630900 is separate from new apparel.
- Confirm CN/TARIC, preferential origin, Portuguese labelling, REACH, GPSR, VAT, packaging EPR and textile EPR for each SKU.
