SAMI BIN AYYAD GROUP – SWEDEN APPAREL MARKET STUDY PACKAGE

Contents:
1) SBA_Sweden_Apparel_Market_Study_AR.docx – Arabic market study.
2) SBA_Sweden_Apparel_Market_Data.xlsx – market indicators, supplier trends, HS6 product data, workwear, used clothing HS630900, prospects, compliance and sources.

Data basis:
- CBI/Eurostat market indicators: 2023.
- HS610910 and HS630900: 2024 WITS/UN Comtrade.
- Workwear: 2024 CBI/Eurostat.
- Prospects are potential outreach targets, not confirmed buyers.

Important:
- HS610910 is one tariff line, not the total T-shirt market.
- Used clothing HS630900 is separate from new apparel HS61/HS62.
- Textile EPR implementation in Sweden is under development; recheck national legislation before placing products on the market.
- Confirm exact CN/TARIC, origin, labelling, REACH, GPSR and VAT for each SKU.
