IMPORTANT NOTES
- HS610910 and HS610990 are individual new-apparel HS6 lines, not total apparel imports.
- HS630900 is worn clothing and is kept separate from new apparel.
- Egypt recorded USD3.12k in Senegal's 2024 HS610910 imports.
- Customs portal states DPI at FOB XAF1m and AV at XAF3m, while May 2024 customs notice
  generalized DPI at FOB XAF500k and all personalized containers. Confirm current rule.
- PVI/Cotecna inspection thresholds and exemptions must be checked for each shipment.
- Senegal is in ECOWAS/UEMOA; Egypt is not. Do not assume regional tariff preference.
- Target entities are channel leads and directory entries, not confirmed active buyers.
- Commercial research only; not legal/customs advice.

Full source URLs are in the report and workbook.
