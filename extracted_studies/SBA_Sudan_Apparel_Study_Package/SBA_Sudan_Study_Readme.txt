Critical interpretation notes
- HS61 and HS62 2025 values are Egypt-reported exports to Sudan from UN Comtrade; they are NOT Sudan-reported imports and do not establish Egypt's share of Sudan's apparel imports.
- Qoovee Apparel & Textile and Textile categories are broader/different product scopes. Do not treat them as HS61+HS62.
- No current product-level Sudan HS8 customs duty or definitive VAT rate is asserted. Verify with the Sudan customs tariff portal and a local customs broker/tax adviser before quotation.
- Buyer names in trade-data platforms are unqualified leads, not verified customers. Zemo Fashion is a public website lead only.
- Scenario price is a user-editable placeholder, not a market quote or forecast.
- Do not ship on open account. Verify importer registration, bank/payment route, security, route, insurance, and customs documentation before production and dispatch.
