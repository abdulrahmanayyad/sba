Important data notes:
- HS61 and HS62 2024 totals are customs chapter values from UN Comtrade.
- Lebanon imports from Egypt HS61 are USD 1.95m in 2024; HS62 bilateral value was not verified from the same reporter extract and is not invented.
- HS610910 Egypt share is calculated from the same WITS reporter data.
- Apparel and footwear market value USD 531m is a Trade.gov estimate including footwear; it is not the HS61+62 total.
- Product HS6204/6110/6114 data are CEPII-BACI secondary datasets.
- Target buyers are prospects, not confirmed customers.
- Customs tariff examples show 5% on many HS61/62 lines; exact line and minimum per kg must be checked. VAT on imports is 11%.
- Lebanese payment, currency and political risks require strict payment security.
