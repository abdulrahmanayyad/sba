SAMI BIN AYYAD GROUP — BAHRAIN APPAREL MARKET STUDY
Files:
1. SBA_Bahrain_Apparel_Market_Study.docx — Arabic-first detailed market study.
2. SBA_Bahrain_Apparel_Trade_Buyers_Compliance.xlsx — trade baseline, Egypt share, HS6 product data, buyer accounts, compliance and source register.

Important data notes:
- HS61 and HS62 values and Egypt bilateral values use 2024 UN Comtrade series.
- HS610910 and HS610510 product-level Egyptian values/quantities are shown where available.
- Women's apparel HS6204 comes from CEPII BACI via EximDataX; it is not the same as the HS62 chapter total.
- Apparel-only retail revenue is not stated where no open verifiable figure was available.
- Buyer groups are prospects, not confirmed customers.
- Bahrain VAT is 10%; customs duty is generally 5% on CIF for most imported goods, subject to exact tariff classification and exceptions.
