SAMI BIN AYYAD GROUP – ROMANIA APPAREL MARKET STUDY PACKAGE

Contents:
1) SBA_Romania_Apparel_Market_Study_AR.docx – Arabic market study.
2) SBA_Romania_Apparel_Market_Data.xlsx – market indicators, HS6 product imports, outerwear/workwear, used clothing HS630900, prospects, compliance checklist and sources.

Data basis:
- Eurostat SITC 84 imports: 2024; broader classification, not identical to HS61+HS62.
- HS610910, HS620342, HS610342 and HS630900: WITS/UN Comtrade, 2024.
- Outerwear: CBI/Eurostat latest market tables.
- Retail prospects are potential outreach targets, not confirmed buyers.

Important:
- Romania is also a clothing manufacturing/export base; assess some local firms as potential partners or competitors, not automatically as buyers.
- Used clothing HS630900 is separate from new apparel.
- Do not use HS392620 or HS401590 as substitutes for textile apparel HS61/HS62.
- Confirm CN/TARIC, preferential origin, labelling, REACH, GPSR, VAT, packaging and EPR obligations for each SKU.
