SAMI BIN AYYAD GROUP – ITALY APPAREL MARKET STUDY PACKAGE
Contents:
1) SBA_Italy_Apparel_Market_Study_AR.docx – Arabic market study.
2) SBA_Italy_Apparel_Market_Data.xlsx – market indicators, suppliers, product opportunities, used clothing HS630900, HS6 example, prospect list, compliance checklist and sources.

Data basis:
- Main apparel market indicators: 2024, based on CBI analysis of Eurostat.
- Used clothing: HS 630900, 2024, WITS/UN Comtrade.
- Product category figures explicitly marked where they combine Italy and Spain; do not attribute combined values to Italy alone.
- Prospects are potential outreach targets, not confirmed buyers or active purchase leads.

Important:
- Confirm CN/TARIC classification, customs duty and preferential origin rules for each exact SKU before quoting.
- No universal apparel tariff rate is stated.
- Used clothing HS630900 is not included in new clothing HS61/HS62.
- Italian fibre-composition labelling and applicable EU product safety/chemical requirements must be checked for each product.
