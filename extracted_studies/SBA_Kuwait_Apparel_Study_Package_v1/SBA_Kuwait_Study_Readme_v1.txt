KEY FINDINGS
- 2023 HS61 imports: USD 844.679m.
- 2023 HS62 imports: USD 602.057m.
- Combined 2023 customs imports: USD 1.446736bn.
- Egypt share of combined 2023 imports: approximately 1.00%.
- 2024 HS620322 cotton men's ensembles: Kuwait imports USD 4.805m; Egypt supplied USD 3.600m (approximately 74.9%).
- 2024 HS610910 cotton T-shirts: Kuwait imports USD 338.036m / 55.776m pieces; Egypt supplied USD 2.214m / 365,356 pieces.
- Kuwait's 2024 official SITC84 imports were KWD 655.262m. This is broader than HS61+62 and is not directly interchangeable.
- GAFTA preference is conditional on the relevant origin rule and certificate. Confirm the exact HS-specific rule before pricing.
- Kuwait's VAT has not been implemented as of the cited 2026 sources.
- Retail prices are consumer-facing and are not wholesale or FOB quotations.

LIMITATIONS
- Full matched 2024/2025 HS61+HS62 chapter totals were not verified in a single harmonized source.
- Buyer accounts are prospects, not confirmed importers or customers.
- Exact current tariff must be confirmed at Kuwait's national HS8/HS10 line.
- No comprehensive domestic garment-manufacturing capacity estimate was established.
- HS611120 babywear quantity is reported in kg and is not converted into pieces.
- Freight figures are dynamic indicative quotes, not binding offers.
