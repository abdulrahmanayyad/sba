Important research limitations:
- A unified, consistent public total for Libya HS61+HS62 imports could not be validated. No aggregate or total Egypt share is invented.
- Product data use CEPII-BACI (HS 2017, release 202601), reconciled from UN Comtrade; exporter-reported data are labelled separately.
- Egypt share: HS6204 24%; HS6203 7.1%; HS6205 11%; HS6109 1.2%; HS6104 0.4%; HS6114 16%; HS6217 53%.
- Egypt 2025 HS62 exports to Libya ($42.56m) are Egypt reporter data and not directly comparable to Libya reporter-side imports.
- Libyan Customs publishes the 2022 tariff; many detailed HS61/62 apparel lines show 5%. Verify later amendments and other charges per HS8.
- Decision 465/2026 creates PTS registration with transition and 2027 thresholds; Decision 449/2026 regulates banking channels for trade supply.
- No unsupported retail-market size or assumed VAT rate has been inserted.
- Importers are prospects based on public self-descriptions; verify registration, beneficial ownership, PTS, bank and credit before contracting.
