IMPORTANT NOTES
- HS6204 women's clothing import figure USD32.6m is CEPII-BACI HS2017.
- HS610910 WITS figure uses H0 classification. Do not combine the series without harmonization.
- ANSTAT used-clothing indicator is shown in portal monetary units; confirm scale and currency
  from downloadable raw data before conversion. It is not new clothing imports.
- CET rates are 0/5/10/20/35; additional levies include RS, PCS, PCC, PUA and VAT.
- FDI threshold: FOB XAF500,000. BSC is mandatory for sea imports.
- VoC/CoC applicability and threshold must be confirmed for each exact product.
- Buyer profiles are network leads, not verified importers.
- Commercial research only; not legal/customs advice.

Full sources and URLs appear in the report and workbook.
