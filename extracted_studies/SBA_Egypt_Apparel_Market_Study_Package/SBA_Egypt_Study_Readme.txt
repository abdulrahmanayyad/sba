METHODOLOGY & IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- The 2024 and 2025 ready-made garment export figures are from the Apparel Export Council
  and GOEIC sources cited in the report.
- The broader 2025 textile/RMG trade total includes ready-made garments, spinning/weaving
  and furnishings. It must not be confused with ready-made garment exports alone.
- No unsupported aggregate value for Egyptian imports under all HS61+HS62 headings
  has been inserted. Product-level trade analysis must be completed from a consistent
  HS revision and reporter/partner dataset before making market-share claims.
- Target accounts are prospects or industry participants, not confirmed active buyers.
  Verify legal entity, current activity, sourcing route and procurement contact before outreach.
- Tariffs, origin rules, ACI/NAFEZA requirements, labeling and standards must be checked
  for the exact HS code, product composition, destination and shipment date.
- The study is commercial research, not legal, tax or customs advice.
