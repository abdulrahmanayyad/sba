Important:
- 2024 HS61 and HS62 values and Egypt bilateral values are based on UN Comtrade series.
- Product-level HS6 data are shown only where available; do not extrapolate them to entire chapters.
- Qatar apparel-only retail revenue was not stated where no open reliable number was verified.
- Qatar National Planning Council and Qatar Open Data provide deeper HS8/HS12 and re-export datasets for further extraction.
- Target buyer groups are prospects, not confirmed customers.
- Qatar has not applied VAT according to the General Tax Authority guide reviewed; customs duties remain applicable.
