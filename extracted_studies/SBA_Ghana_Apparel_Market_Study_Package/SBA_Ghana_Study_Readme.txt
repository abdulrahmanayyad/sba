IMPORTANT DATA NOTES
- HS610910 imports: USD 3.444m in 2024 and USD 5.029m in 2025 according to WITS pages.
- HS610990 imports: USD 7.095m in 2024. These are individual HS6 lines, not total apparel imports.
- WITS pages identify HS nomenclature H0 for some 2024 trade data. Other datasets may use HS2017
  or BACI reconciled trade data and may report materially different totals. Do not mix datasets.
- Egypt recorded USD 6.09k in Ghana's 2024 HS610910 imports.
- Used-clothing market figures refer to used textiles, not new apparel imports.
- CET bands are 0/5/10/20/35%; exact duty and all levies must be calculated for exact HS code.
- Target accounts are prospects, local manufacturers, or channels; not all are confirmed importers.
- Confirm all import documentation and garment-specific labeling with GRA/GSA and a licensed broker.

Sources and URLs are listed in the Word report and Excel Source Register.
