SAMI BIN AYYAD GROUP – BELGIUM APPAREL MARKET STUDY PACKAGE

Contents:
1) SBA_Belgium_Apparel_Market_Study_AR.docx – Arabic market study.
2) SBA_Belgium_Apparel_Market_Data.xlsx – market indicators, HS6 product imports, outerwear/workwear, used clothing HS630900, prospects, compliance checklist and sources.

Data basis:
- CBI/Eurostat apparel market: 2024, HS61+HS62 definition used by CBI.
- Eurostat SITC 84: 2024; broader/different classification.
- HS610910, HS620342 and HS630900: WITS/UN Comtrade, 2024.
- Outerwear/workwear: latest CBI/Eurostat tables.
- Prospects are potential outreach targets, not confirmed buyers.

Important:
- Belgium apparel import value declined during 2019–2024; market is large but not a high-growth market in this period.
- Do not treat SITC 84 as identical to HS61+HS62.
- Used clothing HS630900 is separate from new apparel.
- Verify CN/TARIC, origin, labelling languages, REACH, GPSR, VAT, packaging EPR and textile EPR for each SKU.
