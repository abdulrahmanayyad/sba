IMPORTANT NOTES
- 2024 HS6 trade data are individual tariff lines, not total HS61/62 apparel imports.
- Egypt's HS610910 recorded imports into Kenya were USD70.68k in 2024.
- Mitumba figures refer to used clothing and must not be conflated with new apparel imports.
- Kenya's KEBS PVoC service arrangements changed in February 2026; check the latest KEBS
  notice and inspection provider status before shipment.
- CET, VAT, IDF and RDL rates/exemptions must be confirmed for the exact HS code and
  shipment date.
- EPZ companies are potential partners/industry actors, not confirmed buyers of SBA products.
- This is commercial research, not legal/customs advice.
