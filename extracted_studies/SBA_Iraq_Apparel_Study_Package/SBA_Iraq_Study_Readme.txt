Critical notes:
- Iraq 2024 official COSIT import methodology excludes Kurdistan Region; reporter and mirror data can differ substantially.
- HS61 imports from Egypt were only about USD 167.4k; HS62 imports from Egypt were USD 4.45m, mostly men's suits/jackets.
- COSIT/WITS reported cotton T-shirt imports at USD 10.79m and other textile T-shirts at USD 12.75m in 2024.
- Women's apparel HS6204 secondary dataset values conflict with chapter-level HS62 totals; not used as an official market figure.
- New Cabinet Decision 957/2025 tariff schedule applies in 2026; HSC portal shows general apparel/knitwear category at 15%, but exact HS8 line and exceptions must be checked.
- CoC applies only to regulated products; confirm exact clothing HS/product scope with COSQC-approved body.
- Buyer prospects include self-described importers; legal status and creditworthiness must be checked before contracting.
