OVERVIEW & STRATEGIC HIGHLIGHTS:
This official research package contains the comprehensive market study, macroeconomic
indicators, mirror trade datasets, regulatory compliance guides, and logistics route
assessments for the Republic of the Sudan apparel and textiles sector.

Key Statistical Baselines:
- Ready-Made Garments Imports Indicator (CBOS 2025 Report): $72,203,000 USD
  * Rebounding +83.9% from 2024 ($39.25M), remains 52.4% below 2022 ($151.78M)
- Combined Egyptian Apparel Exports (WITS Mirror Trade 2024): $50,051,000 USD
  * Woven Apparel (HS 62): $38,508,000 USD (Egypt ranks #1 globally to Sudan, +82.9% YoY)
  * Knitted Apparel (HS 61): $11,543,000 USD (Egypt ranks #2 globally behind China)
- Domestic Value Chain Shock:
  * Sudan raw cotton exports collapsed from $384.0M (2022) to $64.0M (2025)
  * Total paralysis of domestic ginning and spinning mills creates captive import demand
- Regulatory & Banking Mandate:
  * Central Bank of Sudan Circular No. 02/2026 (6 Jan 2026)
  * Mandatory Electronic Import Permit (Baladna) and Electronic IM Form
  * Strict prohibition on advance cash payments for garments
  * Settlements restricted to Confirmed Irrevocable Letters of Credit (L/C) or Bank CAD
- Trade Accords:
  * COMESA: 0.0% customs duty with 35% local ex-factory value addition
  * GAFTA: 0.0% preferential duty with Arab Certificate of Origin
  * Strict ban on commercial importation of used clothing (secondhand / balah)
- Logistics & Incoterms:
  * Maritime: Red Sea ocean feeder transit to Port Sudan (3-6 days)
  * Overland: Argeen Border Crossing via Upper Egypt highway (4-8 days)
  * Delivery risk strictly terminated at CFR Port Sudan or CPT Argeen terminal
