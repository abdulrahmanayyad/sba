OVERVIEW & STRATEGIC HIGHLIGHTS:
This official research package contains the comprehensive market study, trade data,
industrial partner directories, tariff frameworks, landed cost simulators, and risk
management matrices for the Republic of Tunisia apparel and textiles sector.

Key Statistical Baselines:
- Total Documented Apparel Imports (HS 61 + HS 62): $418,396,000 USD (2023)
  * Knitted Apparel (HS 61): $148,135,000 USD (35.41%)
  * Woven Apparel (HS 62): $270,261,000 USD (64.59%)
- Imports from Egypt: $1,290,030 USD (~0.31% share - Massive growth headroom)
- APII Industrial Census: 1,323 textile/apparel enterprises (>=10 workers)
  * 1,075 Totally Exporting Enterprises (Régime TE - 81.25%)
  * 248 Non-totally exporting enterprises (Régime OTE - 18.75%)
  * >150,000 Direct Industrial Workers (~30% of total manufacturing workforce)
- Tunisia EU Export Leadership (CETTEX / Eurostat 2024):
  * #1 Leading Supplier of Professional Workwear to the EU (€317.7M EUR, 17.44% share)
  * #4 Leading Supplier of Denim / Jeanswear to the EU (8.21% share)
- Trade Advantages:
  * 0% Duty under GAFTA & 1998 Bilateral Agreement (vs. 30%-50% MFN tariffs for Asia)
  * Euro-Med Full Cumulation of Origin under Agadir Agreement (Egyptian fabric + Tunisian CMT -> 0% into EU)
