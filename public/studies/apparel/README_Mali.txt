OVERVIEW & STRATEGIC HIGHLIGHTS:
This official research package contains the comprehensive market intelligence study,
trade data baselines, product opportunity matrices, buyer prospect directories,
UEMOA tariff frameworks, and logistics risk assessments for the Republic of Mali.

Key Statistical Baselines:
- Total Documented New Apparel Imports (HS 61 + HS 62): $12,400,110 USD (2023 WITS)
  * Knitted Apparel (HS 61): $8,313,610 USD (67.04%)
  * Woven Apparel (HS 62): $4,086,500 USD (32.96%)
- Leading Supplying Countries:
  * China ($3.68M in HS61, $1.99M in HS62) & Hong Kong ($2.61M in HS61)
  * Regional intra-trade: Senegal ($591K in HS61, $75K in HS62)
- Egypt's Baseline Position:
  * HS61: $29,740 USD | HS62: $660 USD | Combined: $30,400 USD (~0.245% share)
  * Caution: Mali-reported import statistics; reflects early-stage commercial presence.
- Second-Hand Clothing Competition (HS 6309):
  * Import Value: $17,015,240 USD (34,371,400 kg)
  * Exceeds new apparel imports by +37.2%, driving intense price competition.
- Domestic Cotton Ecosystem:
  * CMDT (est. 1974) operates 18 ginning plants; >95% lint exported raw.
  * Upstream ginning must not be conflated with downstream finished apparel manufacturing.
  * CMDT is an agricultural producer, NOT an apparel buyer.
- Trade Policy & Compliance:
  * UEMOA Common External Tariff (20% to 35% on finished goods).
  * Standard VAT: 18%. AES Community Levy: 0.5% on non-AES imports.
  * Mali withdrawal from ECOWAS effective 29 Jan 2025; remains in UEMOA.
  * Preliminary Documentary Verification (VDP) applies to imports > CFAF 1,000,000 FOB.
  * BESC electronic cargo tracking mandatory for maritime transit.
- Logistics & Security:
  * Landlocked; primary maritime transit via Dakar (Senegal) and Abidjan (Côte d'Ivoire).
  * World Bank approved funding in May 2025 for Bamako-Dakar Northern Corridor.
  * Security advisory mandates remote-first qualification and licensed Bamako partners.
