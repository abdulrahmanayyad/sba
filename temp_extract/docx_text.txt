Global trade baseline | قاعدة التجارة العالمية      UN Comtrade is the primary reference for merchandise trade by reporter, partner, HS code, flow and period. HS Chapter 61 covers knitted/crocheted apparel; HS Chapter 62 covers non-knitted/non-crocheted apparel. In 2024, global trade recorded in the cited dataset was approximately US$244bn for Chapter 61 and US$223bn for Chapter 62. These are trade values, not retail sales or factory-addressable demand.          Top Chapter 61 exporters in 2024 included China, Bangladesh, Vietnam, Türkiye, Cambodia, Italy and India.          Top Chapter 62 exporters in 2024 included China, Bangladesh, Vietnam, Italy, Türkiye, India and Morocco.          Top import markets in the cited 2024 dataset included the United States, Germany, France, Japan, United Kingdom, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, Poland, South Korea, Canada and Australia.          UN Comtrade public preview is limited; full country-by-country product/partner extraction should use the official API or downloadable datasets, with release dates and revisions recorded.         Methodology and data discipline | المنهجية وضبط البيانات          Primary trade source: UN Comtrade; cross-check with World Bank WITS and national statistical/customs agencies.          Do not combine retail market revenue, customs import value, re-exports, domestic production and wholesale sales in one metric.          Compare countries only when product scope, HS revision, flow, period, partner scope and valuation basis match.          Report customs value in USD or local currency, quantity in net weight and supplementary quantity only where the reporting country supplies it. Never convert kilograms to pieces without a product-specific, documented average garment weight.          Use HS61 and HS62 at chapter level for market screening, then HS6/HS8 lines for product-level decisions. Product examples include 6109 T-shirts/vests, 6104 women’s knitted garments, 6105 men’s knitted shirts, 6110 jerseys/pullovers, 6203 men’s suits/trousers, 6204 women’s suits/trousers, 6205 men’s shirts and 6206 women’s blouses/shirts. Validate current HS revision and national tariff line.          Market-size estimates from commercial research providers are not treated as official trade data. Any retail-market estimate must show provider, year, scope and methodology.          Buyer lists identify potential accounts, not confirmed purchasers or active procurement mandates. Verify the legal entity, buying office, category scope, supplier onboarding route and current contact before outreach.         SBA Group positioning | التموضع التنافسي للمجموعة      The recommended positioning is not lowest-price competition against the largest Asian sourcing countries. SBA should sell a combination of Egyptian manufacturing capability, product development, flexible order architecture, dependable quality, short decision loops, and repeatable replenishment. The offer should be segmented by buyer type:          Retail groups and private-label owners: seasonal ranges, fit/sample development, audit readiness, production planning and repeat orders.          Wholesalers and importers: focused SKU packs, stable quality, clear size curves, consolidated shipment and protected payment terms.          Brands and sourcing offices: CM/FOB/OEM capability, technical packs, fabric options, lab dips, testing and traceability.          Regional distributors: own-brand capsules and replenishment basics, with local-market pricing and language-compliant labels.         Egypt-origin trade advantages | مزايا المنشأ المصري          EU-Egypt Association Agreement: industrial products covered by the agreement can benefit from tariff elimination, subject to product-specific rules of origin and documentary compliance.          UK-Egypt Association Agreement: preferential arrangements and rules of origin apply; use the UK tariff tool and the applicable product-specific rule.          Egypt-Türkiye FTA: in force since 1 March 2007; industrial goods originating in Egypt benefit from tariff elimination subject to the agreement’s rules of origin.          GAFTA: potentially relevant for qualifying Arab-origin products; verify the product-specific origin rule, value-added calculation and required certificate.          Egypt QIZ: a special US duty-free route for qualifying goods from designated Egyptian QIZ factories with prescribed Israeli/US content calculations. It is not a blanket benefit for all Egyptian factories or products.
