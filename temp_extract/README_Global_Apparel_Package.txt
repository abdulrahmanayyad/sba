SBA GROUP — GLOBAL APPAREL 41-COUNTRY PACKAGE
Files:
- SBA_Global_Apparel_41_Country_Market_Study.docx — master country study document.
- SBA_Global_Apparel_41_Country_Data_Matrix.xlsx — structured country matrix, HS product map and source register.
- Antigravity_Implementation_Brief_Global_Apparel.txt — implementation instructions.

Coverage: 41 countries across GCC, Levant, North Africa, Europe, Americas and Asia-Pacific.

Important data limitations:
- Several country-level HS61+HS62 totals and all-country HS6/HS8 partner breakdowns were not independently verified in this package.
- The document explicitly marks such fields as pending rather than fabricating values.
- Import value is not retail sales or guaranteed addressable demand.
- Buyer groups are prospect accounts, not confirmed customers.
- Tariffs, VAT and rules of origin require product-specific validation.

Primary references include UN Comtrade, WITS, Eurostat, Egyptian Ready-Made Garments Export Council, European Commission, UK Government, US FTC/CBP and US Trade.gov.
