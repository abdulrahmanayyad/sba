PAGE TEMPLATE — EACH COUNTRY
1. Country hero: country name, flag/icon, one-sentence market proposition, priority label.
2. Executive Summary.
3. Market Overview and Demand.
4. Imports & Exports: HS61 and HS62; separate years and scopes; clearly label unavailable data.
5. Product-Level Trade Intelligence: HS6/HS8 only where verified.
6. Main Supplier Countries and Egypt’s Share: show only sourced figures; no invented shares.
7. Product Opportunities relevant to SBA capabilities.
8. Local Competition and Domestic Production.
9. Potential Buyers & Retail Groups: mark as “Target Accounts — not confirmed customers”.
10. Distribution and Go-to-Market Channels.
11. Import Regulations, Tariffs, VAT, Labelling and Rules of Origin.
12. Logistics and Route-to-Market.
13. Commercial Models: CM, FOB, OEM, Private Label and own brand.
14. Scenario Calculator: 10k / 25k / 50k / 100k units monthly. Mark prices as user-entered assumptions, not market facts.
15. Risks and Entry Recommendations.
16. Sources, data year and methodology.

- Include a visible “Data completeness” field.
- Include the latest available reporting year per market without pretending all markets use the same year.
